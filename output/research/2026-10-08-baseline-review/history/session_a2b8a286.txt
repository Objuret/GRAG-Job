## Answer

Reading it from the code so every formula is the one that actually runs.

The steps are read out of the code. One last check that each formula is exactly what runs, then you get it.

Read from `test/artefact/v4_walk.py`, the one file the arm sorts by.

## What goes in

**From the question**, one querytagger call gives a description, two lists of tags, and five readings per tag. The embedder then gives three sets of cosines:
- each question tag against all 16,609 graph tags,
- the description and the raw question against all 4,808 chunk descriptions,
- each question tag against the description.

**From the graph**, the same for every question:
- 57,204 tag-to-chunk edges, each with five values: topic (cosine of tag against chunk description) and the four facet scores,
- each chunk's product,
- each chunk's near group: the neighbouring parts of the same record or Slack channel.

## The steps

**1. Closeness**, per question tag and graph tag.

`fit = max(0, (cos − median) / (1.4826 × median|cos − median|))`

- Median and spread are taken over that question tag's own 16,609 cosines.
- The unit is spreads above that tag's own background. Top values run from about 5 to 14.

**2. Edge positions**, per edge, the same for every question.

`pos_k = rank of the edge in column k / 57,204`

- One position for each of the five columns, between 0 and 1.

**3. The readings meet the edge.**

```
share = readings / sum of readings
R     = sum over the five of share_k × pos_k     (between 0 and 1)
w     = fit × R / 0.5
```

- Relationship to step 1: multiplied.
- The factor `R / 0.5` runs from 0.05 to 1.97 and is 1.0 at the median.

**4. What the chunk takes from its tags.**

```
v          = the largest w over the chunk's edges, per question tag (0 if it has none)
centrality = cos(question tag, description) / the largest such among the question's tags
             (a negative one counts as 0)
T          = the largest centrality × v over the question's tags
```

- Relationship: a maximum, twice. One question tag and that tag's best edge decide `T`. This pair is "the winning tag" and "the winning edge" below.
- Every other tag of the chunk and every other tag of the question add nothing.

**5. The description.**

```
D  = (cos(description, chunk description) − median) / spread, over the 4,808 chunks
Qs = the same for the raw question
S  = T + max(D, Qs)
```

- Relationship to step 4: added.
- The added part runs from about −3 to +5.

**6. The structure.**

```
lift_P = max(0, trust × (mean S of the product's other chunks − mean S of all chunks))
trust  = tau² / (tau² + sigma² / (group size − 1))
S'     = S + lift_P + lift_N
```

- `tau²` is how much the groups differ from each other; `sigma²` is how much chunks differ inside a group.
- `lift_N` (near group) is the same calculation on what is left of S after the product's mean is taken out. It is measured against the average of that for chunks of the same record kind that sit in a near group (0 if there are none).
- Relationship: added again. The lifts are computed from S itself, so they move with steps 4 and 5 of the other chunks in the group.

**7. The order.**

```
step  = 0.002 / the median spread of the question's tags     (about 0.06)
level = floor((largest S' − S') / step)
```

- Sorted by level.
- Inside a level: chunks that have a winning edge come first, ordered by that edge's four facet positions, in the order of the winning tag's readings. Then chunk id.
- The harness takes chunks from the top until 72,000 characters.

## How the steps hang together

As one line:

`S' = max over question tags [ centrality × max over edges ( fit × R / 0.5 ) ] + max(D, Qs) + lift_P + lift_N`

There are three kinds of join:
- **A multiplication** inside the tag term (steps 1, 3, 4).
- **A maximum** that picks one edge and one question tag.
- **Plain additions** between the tag term, the description and the structure (steps 5, 6).

`S'` decides the order. The facet order of step 7 only decides between chunks that land in the same level.

   - **Closeness as a standing** (`standing`): how far a graph tag's closeness sits above the middle of that question tag's own list, in units of the list's spread. Below the middle counts as 0.
   - **The pick:** graph tags scoring 0.9212 or more against the question tag. A question tag that picks nothing is left as it is.
   - **The facets rank the picked** (`ranked_closeness`, `facet_order`): the picked connections are ordered by the four facet classes, in the order of that question tag's readings, then by topic, then by closeness. They are then placed evenly between the closest tag's score and the level, and that placed score is turned into a standing the same way.
   - **No facet multiplier:** a connection's weight is its closeness.
   - **Per chunk, the best connection carries it** (`chunk_values`, `best_edges`).
4. **Across the question tags** (`centrality`, `tag_value`): each question tag counts by its closeness to the description, relative to the largest such closeness among the question's own tags. The chunk takes the largest of that weight times its best connection. That is the tag term, never below 0.
5. **The text term** (`text_side`): the standing of the description against the chunk's description, and of the raw question against it, each over all chunks. The larger of the two. This one can be negative, so it can pull a chunk down.
6. **Strength** = tag term + text term.
7. **Structure lifts** (`structure_lifts`, `group_lift`), both never negative, and the size of the group is not read:
   - **Product lift:** how far the other chunks of the chunk's product stand above the average chunk.
   - **Near-group lift:** taken on the strength with the product's own average subtracted. How far the other chunks of the near group stand, on that, above what is usual for the chunk's record kind.
8. **Order** (`levels_and_order`, `level_order`): by strength with its lifts, in steps of 0.002 of closeness, expressed in spreads. Inside one step: chunks that have a winning connection first, then by the four facet positions of that connection in the winning tag's order, then by chunk id.
9. **The cut** (`_budget_contexts`, `cut_at_budget`): chunks in that order until the text reaches 72,000 characters. The one that crosses the line is cut mid-text.
10. **Answer and judging:** Sonnet writes the answer from those chunks; Haiku judges it.

**Where the named product comes in:** only through the raw question's embedding in step 5 and the product lift in step 7. Nothing matches the name itself, and nothing puts its chunks first.

The chain above is the checked version: each step was compared against the code before I sent it. Nothing is running now.
