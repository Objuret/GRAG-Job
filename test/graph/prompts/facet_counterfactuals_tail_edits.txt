
# Output

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else.

{"edges": [
  {"t": "<the phrase, copied exactly as given>",
   "temporal":     {"edits": [{"find": "<verbatim span of the text>", "replace": "<replacement>"}], "note": "<one sentence: what contribution you neutralized>"},
   "why":          {"edits": [...], "note": "..."},
   "activity":     {"edits": [...], "note": "..."},
   "concreteness": {"edits": [...], "note": "..."}}
]}

One object per phrase, every phrase given, all four facets on each.

Rules for `find`:

* it must be a character-for-character substring of the text exactly as given, including
  punctuation, casing and internal spacing;
* it must occur exactly once in the text; if the wording you want to change occurs more than
  once, extend the span with surrounding text until it is unique;
* it must not span a line break unless the change genuinely needs to;
* edits within one facet must not overlap each other.

Rules for `replace`:

* the replacement is what that span becomes; use "" to delete the span;
* keep the replacement as close to the original as the neutralization allows;
* the result must read as natural text, not as a gap.

`edits` may be the empty list `[]` when the facet contributes nothing to that phrase's
relevance. Then `note` says why there was nothing to neutralize.

Copy each phrase into `t` exactly as it was given to you. Do not add phrases, drop phrases,
re-order them, correct them, or merge them.
