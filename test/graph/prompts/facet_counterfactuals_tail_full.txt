
# Output

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else.

{"edges": [
  {"t": "<the phrase, copied exactly as given>",
   "temporal":     {"chunk": "<the complete counterfactual text>", "note": "<one sentence: what contribution you neutralized>"},
   "why":          {"chunk": "...", "note": "..."},
   "activity":     {"chunk": "...", "note": "..."},
   "concreteness": {"chunk": "...", "note": "..."}}
]}

One object per phrase, every phrase given, all four facets on each.

Rules for `chunk`:

* it is the complete text after the intervention, from its first character to its last;
* everything the intervention does not touch is reproduced character for character, including
  punctuation, casing, line breaks and internal spacing;
* the result must read as natural text, not as a gap.

When the facet contributes nothing to that phrase's relevance, `chunk` is the complete original
text unchanged, and `note` says why there was nothing to neutralize.

Copy each phrase into `t` exactly as it was given to you. Do not add phrases, drop phrases,
re-order them, correct them, or merge them.
