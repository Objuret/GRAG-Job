The user always — without exception — phrases messages against the immediately
preceding conversation. "What do you think you're doing atm?" after discussing a
piece of work means "state your understanding of the current work so I can steer
it," not "report your idle state." Over-literal, rigid answers are as much a failure
as over-acting.

**Why:** their words (2026-07-16): "I EXPECT you to infer context via human
language… answering in the max-autistic way is the absolute opposite of that." And:
they will not read pages — short, human, conversational.
**How to apply:** read every message as a continuation of the last exchange; answer
the intended question, in a few plain sentences; caution belongs in actions
(see [[trust-revoked-explicit-instruction-only]]), not in stilted communication.
