# Retrieval construction: verified outcome

The best observed fixed rule for the requested total source-ID objective is
`directed_frontier_0004`: 2,015 gold-source hits at the same 72,000 serialized
characters per question. Its mean per-question recall is 0.533131. The best
mean-recall rule is `query_priority_best-macro-recall_lexicographic_first`:
1,954 hits and 0.536217. The canonical construction reference has 1,059 hits
and 0.295595. These totals sum question/source links, not globally unique sources.

The bounded structural investigation is complete: all 18 declared populations
are complete on the original 95 cases and independently verified. The final
selection is in `output/research/2026-09-24-structural-selection-v3/`. Its two
selection records are byte-identical to v2, whose fresh 99-case replay is preserved
and hash-linked by `replay-provenance.json`. The final Product-path recovery
comparison does not change either leader. This is completion of the stated
component/relationship investigation, not exhaustive proof of optimal retrieval.

## What was structurally tested

| Component or relationship | Executed alternatives |
| --- | --- |
| Tag paths and evidence combination | Maximum/mean/sum across graph tags; query maximum/mean/minimum; same-edge versus separately combined facet evidence; reduction before edges, before traversal or afterward |
| Recruitment | Joint, independent facets, queries and routes; best-rank/all-stream/shared-visited nomination; chunk recruitment feeding traversal; graph-tag recruitment before chunks; first/all supporters and admission ordering |
| Query-relative facet priority | Scalar facet evidence versus ordered per-query facet keys, crossed with sponsor policy and downstream joint/facet contexts |
| Graph shape and traversal | Adjacency and shared channel/product/employee groups; parallel/sequential paths; direct/graph inclusive, exclusive and intersection use; forward/reverse/symmetric management links; one through deeper walks up to 32; seed reinsertion versus latest-step feedback |
| Descriptions | Tag matching, initial evidence, destination scoring, independent recruitment and disabled roles; per-step versus post-walk description gates |
| Scope | Saved graph-grounded areas versus query-evidence peaks and graph reach; union/intersection; name/route/binding joins; scope before/after traversal and equal-depth/area-first/area-only admission |
| Ranking and assembly | Alternative within-tier evidence rules; exact-record recovery off/late/before nomination/before traversal; component-contiguous ordering; fixed external 72k cut |
| Interactions | Matching with ordering, scope with graph/recruitment, recruitment with traversal, tag recruitment with management routes, query-relative priority with management routes, record recovery with reachable graph context |

The coverage ledger distinguishes implemented policies from actually exercised
behaviour. For example, 96 scope policies produce only five distinct mask
sequences on this population; these are not 96 independently observed behaviours.

The final record comparison initially produced identical rankings in all five
modes on the leading Product–Channel contexts. Graph inspection showed those
contexts exclude all 569 multiple-record chunks. The additive Product-route
comparison reaches those chunks and changes rankings in all five modes across
the population. Its 1,045 deliveries independently verify, and all 190 unchanged
controls reproduce the prior engine. Turning late recovery off raises hits from
1,742 to 1,770 for the tag context and 1,590 to 1,612 for the priority context.
Both improve five cases and lose none. Earlier recovery and contiguous assembly
can change rankings/deliveries without changing source-ID totals. These results
support context dependence, not universally disabling recovery.

## What the total-hit construction does

1. Independently recruit graph tags for the query-tag streams, using all
   supporting streams and reciprocal tag-tier evidence before edges become chunks.
2. Combine tag-name and tag-description matching with query and edge facet
   evidence. Keep facets separate during maximum aggregation across graph tags;
   average query evidence before graph propagation.
3. Add the existing channel-bound management route in parallel with the seed,
   taking maximum evidence, before the existing two-step Product–Channel walk.
   Apply the query-description gate at each graph step.
4. Require both direct and graph support. Combine the facet streams, apply the
   whole-query description at the destination, and nominate jointly with the
   saved query-derived area receiving admission priority.
5. Apply the existing exact-record recovery and deterministic ordering. Resolve
   source bodies and apply the fixed serialized-character prefix only afterward;
   the partial boundary receives no additional source-ID credit.

The exact executable JSON, rather than this prose, defines the selected rule.
Gold, source bodies and source costs do not enter its retrieval computation.

The management addition raises the simpler tag-frontier parent's 2,008 hits to
2,015 entirely on case_091 (0 to 7 of 12); the other 94 hit counts tie. Forward,
reverse and symmetric variants tie on aggregate scores. This is not evidence
that management direction itself explains the improvement. The simpler 2,008
rule is the one exercised by the completed real answer-generation smoke.

The macro leader instead uses each query's facet priorities as ordered keys
for graph-tag recruitment and retains earliest-tier sponsors. Its downstream
two-step construction is shared. Against that rule, the total-hit leader wins
23 original cases, loses 22 and ties 50: 61 additional hits do not mean universal
improvement.

## Answer quality is a separate result

The completed ten-question smoke used the simpler 2,008-hit rule and generated
ten fresh answers. It produced 139 successful metric cells and one faithfulness
error. Same-ten saved baseline comparison:

| Metric | Lucene | Vector | Artefact smoke |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Source-ID context recall | 0.118085 | 0.347832 | 0.562171 |
| Answer correctness | 0.157411 | 0.230033 | 0.181971 |

Thus the artefact retrieves more cited source IDs but has lower answer
correctness than vector on these ten questions. The baselines are historical
runs with matching question/corpus hashes, budget and model names, not a fresh
simultaneous evaluation. Full metrics and the preserved failed first attempt
are documented in `2026-09-24-real-gold-smoke-results.md`.

The non-LLM context string metrics compare graph chunks with entire reference
artifacts. Their length-based comparability limitation is documented separately
in `2026-09-24-nonllm-metric-contract.md`; it does not explain away answer
correctness or change any reported score.

## Evidence limits

These are fixed rules selected using development gold on repeatedly reused
cases, not held-out generalization evidence, per-query oracle choices or proof
of a global optimum. Structural alternatives cover the named component roles
and important interactions; finite catalogs do not enumerate every possible
program. Existing interpretation and one exact-name grounding branch remain
fixed; query-evidence scope discovery was tested independently. Finite traversal
depth comparisons do not prove convergence.
