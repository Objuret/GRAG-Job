## Research basis

Adaptive reuse can overfit even a nominal holdout: Dwork et al.,
[Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2015/hash/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Abstract.html).
This check closes an adaptive loop; it does not implement that paper's privacy
mechanism or inherit its guarantees.

Incomplete relevance judgments can distort retrieval evaluation: Buckley and
Voorhees, [Retrieval Evaluation with Incomplete Information](https://www.nist.gov/publications/retrieval-evaluation-incomplete-information).
Here, pooling delivered sources, recording unknowns and allowing alternative
support limit a concrete measurement error; they do not make the judgments complete.
