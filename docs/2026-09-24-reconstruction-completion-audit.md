## Remaining unknowns are findings, not completed repairs

- Whether the learned graph measurements faithfully express the intended facet relationship. The wording mismatch and limits of label-agreement evidence are established; a corrected measurement is not.
- Whether a scorer using question plus description improves interpretation or retrieval, and how its target should be enforced. Diagnostic approval and inherited input restriction are established; a comparative winner is not.
- Whether exact-only name grounding supersedes the accepted misspelling behavior by a later decision elsewhere. The implementation transition and examined records are established; universal absence of approval is not.
- Whether any tested projection/scope family adequately expresses query-relative areas across the intended cases. Existing behaviors and coverage limits are established; conceptual equivalence is not.
- Whether development-selected retrieval gains generalize or improve answers. The completed smoke does not establish that; later leader preflight supplies no answer-quality result.

No replacement specification, new retrieval policy or experiment is authorized by treating this audit as complete.
