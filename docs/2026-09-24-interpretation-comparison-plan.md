## Concrete candidate clarification

Keep the existing facet lines and numeric response schema for the input-access control. For a separate wording condition, replace the activity line with:

> activity — looking at the activities and processes represented in the described content: how relevant is the tag to that content, seen through this facet?

Append this clarification after the facet definitions:

> Judge the tag's relevance to the described content through each facet. Do not substitute a judgment about whether something is being done to the thing named by the tag. Being discussed or referenced does not by itself establish low relevance through a facet. Do not treat the presence of a date, name or number alone as evidence of a strong relationship. Judge only characteristics supported by the supplied input; do not supply facts from an imagined answer.

This removes the automatic presentation-form contrast without deciding that all discussion is strong activity evidence. The replacement is a candidate interpretation of the user's correction, not proof of equivalence. It does not relabel or refit graph values. No graph prompt replacement is deployed: doing so would require a separately attributable measurement comparison.

The unchanged system prompt keeps the target as described content. A disagreement between question and description cannot be silently counted as a corrected generation: these conditions leave generation fixed. They can reveal sensitivity to additional query information, not prove full interpretation repair.

Use all ten existing smoke cases, identified mechanically from the saved cohort. This is a reused development comparison, not heldout validation. Use two independent SCORE repetitions per condition (80 calls maximum), so variation under identical inputs is visible beside intervention effects. Record the old cached score separately; do not compare only old cached control against fresh candidate and ignore generation-time/model variability. Pin current model, temperature, token limit and actual prompt hashes; retain every response and failure. Never choose the better repetition using gold.

Replay each score condition/repetition through three already-fixed constructions: the earlier joint arm, the highest-total-hit program, and the highest-mean-recall program. These represent different existing dependencies; they are not selected anew from intervention results. Hold the graph, description/tag embeddings, name-grounded area, coefficients, programs and 72k delivery contract fixed within each matched comparison.

Record in order:

1. Facet-value changes, within-tag priority reversals and within-facet tag-order reversals, alongside repeat differences.

3. Direct evidence, propagated evidence, join exclusions, area membership/admission depth and recovery advances.
4. Full chunk order, delivered full chunks, partial boundary and credited source-ID set, joined only after ranking.
5. Source-ID precision/recall and per-case gains/losses, explicitly separate from answer quality. Do not use gold to select repetitions, fix descriptions or tune the programs.

Common exact-control replay is a prerequisite to interpreting differences. The value-only retrieval boundary remains in force. Source text is resolved only for the existing delivery contract; do not expose it to SCORE or export it into reports. Preserve failures as failures; no automatic repeated attempts after uncertain execution.

No RAGAS result exists until fresh answers and judges have run under a separately recorded fixed condition. A numerical replay is not a smoke and a retrieved gold source is not proof of an answered claim.

## What this first comparison leaves open

- Joint one-call interpretation versus split calls, generation revision using the original question, and preservation of compound phrases require a later matched generation comparison. Do not use this fixed-generation test to close those choices.
- Graph-label semantics and query/graph scale alignment remain unresolved even if QD1 improves retrieval. Existing label-agreement audits and conditional-reader failures must remain part of their assessment.
- The three frozen downstream programs provide interaction evidence, not complete structural coverage or a newly sanctioned artefact design.
- Changes in selected-set coverage cannot be inferred merely from the scalar source-ID metric. Reuse existing recorded content diagnostics only with their original attribution and limits; do not add source-derived retrieval information.
